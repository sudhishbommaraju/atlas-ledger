# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 24, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Atlas ("Company," "we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

**Account Information:**
- Name, email address, phone number
- Company name and business information
- Billing and payment information

**Credentials and API Keys:**
- Stripe API keys (encrypted)
- Bank login credentials via Plaid OAuth
- ERP credentials (NetSuite, SAP, etc.)
- These are encrypted immediately and stored securely

**Demo and Testing Data:**
- Data you input during testing or demo use
- Sample API keys you provide for testing

**Communication:**
- Emails, support tickets, and feedback you send
- Chat logs with support team
- Survey responses

### 2.2 Information Automatically Collected

**Usage Data:**
- Pages visited and time spent
- Clicks, searches, and interactions
- Device type, browser, operating system
- IP address and geolocation
- Error logs and crash reports

**Financial Data (via your integrations):**
- Transaction information from Stripe
- Balance data from your bank (via Plaid)
- Payout information from your ERP
- We only collect data necessary for drift detection

**Cookies:**
- Session cookies for authentication
- Preference cookies for theme and language
- Analytics cookies (with your consent)

### 2.3 Third-Party Data

We may receive information from:
- Stripe, Plaid, NetSuite about account status
- Payment processors about transaction status
- Service providers for data verification

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on:
- **Contract:** Necessary to provide the Service
- **Consent:** With your explicit opt-in (analytics, marketing)
- **Legitimate Interest:** Fraud prevention, service improvement, security
- **Legal Obligation:** Compliance with laws and regulations

## 4. How We Use Your Information

### 4.1 Primary Uses

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the Service
- Process transactions and send related information
- Send administrative communications
- Respond to your inquiries and support requests
- Monitor and analyze Service usage and performance
- Detect, prevent, and address fraud and security issues
- Improve, personalize, and expand the Service

### 4.2 Marketing and Communications

We may use your information to:
- Send promotional emails (with your consent)
- Send product updates and announcements
- Request feedback and conduct surveys
- Send service notifications

You can opt-out of marketing communications at any time via:
- Unsubscribe link in emails
- Settings in your account dashboard
- Emailing privacy@atlas.ai

### 4.3 Aggregate and De-identified Data

We may use aggregate or de-identified information to:
- Analyze trends and usage patterns

- Conduct research and benchmarking
- Create statistical insights

This data cannot identify you personally.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Data

Atlas does not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

### 5.2 We Share Data With

We may share information with:

**Service Providers:**
- Cloud hosting providers (AWS, Google Cloud)
- Payment processors (Stripe for billing)
- Email providers for communications
- Analytics platforms (with anonymization)

These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and may only use data to provide services to us.

**Legal Requirements:**
- When required by law (subpoena, court order)
- To protect rights, privacy, and safety
- To enforce our agreements
- To protect against fraudulent or illegal activity

**Business Transfers:**
- In case of acquisition, merger, or asset sale
- Your data may be transferred as part of the transaction
- You will be notified before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy

**Your Explicit Consent:**
- When you authorize us to share data with specific third parties
- For integrations you explicitly enable

### 5.3 We Do NOT Share

We never share with third parties:
- Your API keys or credentials (even in encrypted form)
- Your financial transaction details
- Your audit logs or drift detection history
- Your internal balance or reserve information

## 6. Data Security

### 6.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- TLS 1.2+ encryption for data in transit
- Secure key management and rotation
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Intrusion detection and prevention systems
- Firewalls and DDoS protection

### 6.2 Limitations

While we implement robust security, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee:
- Absolute protection against all attacks
- Prevention of all unauthorized access
- Protection if credentials are compromised due to your actions

If you suspect a security breach, contact security@atlas.ai immediately.

## 7. Data Retention

### 7.1 Retention Periods

We retain data as follows:

**Account Data:** Retained as long as your account is active, plus 30 days after termination

**Transaction Data:** Retained for 7 years (for financial and audit requirements)

**API Keys:** Encrypted and retained until you disconnect the integration

**Audit Logs:** Retained for minimum 7 years (regulatory requirement)

**Cookies:** Session cookies deleted upon logout; preference cookies retained for 2 years

**Backups:** Retained for 30 days before deletion

### 7.2 Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of non-essential data by contacting privacy@atlas.ai. We will delete data within 30 days, except:
- Data required by law or regulation
- Data necessary for legal disputes
- Aggregated or de-identified data

## 8. Your Data Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have:

**Right of Access:** Request a copy of your personal data

**Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data

**Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)

**Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we use your data

**Right to Data Portability:** Receive your data in a portable format

**Right to Object:** Opt-out of certain processing activities

**Right to Lodge Complaint:** File complaints with your data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@atlas.ai with:
- Your name and email
- Description of your request
- Proof of identity

We will respond within 30 days (or as required by law).
